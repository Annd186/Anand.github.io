This beautiful illumination below here is called Keyakizaka Street, which is next to the Roppongi Hills shopping centre. This 400 metre illumination is lighting up with 930,000 sparkling white and blue LEDs until Christmas Day. From the pedestrian bridge near the Roppongi Hills Arena, you'll get a stunning view of the trees covered in shimmering lights, with Tokyo Tower in the background, from 5pm to 11pm daily. Perfect night time stroll with your beloved one.
